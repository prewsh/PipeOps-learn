03

Status without colour

Every state is a 6px marker plus a word. Filled = done. Ring = in flight. Hollow = not started. Hollow with a bar = locked. Half-filled = attention. A slash suffix = late. The same chip works in the mobile app, the admin table and a plain-text email.

06

Rules of the system

01
One dominant action per screen

Everything else is secondary, tertiary or a link. If two things look primary, the screen has failed.

02
Deadlines are always double

Absolute date with an explicit (WAT) label, plus a relative countdown. Never one without the other.

03
Nothing blocks, everything nudges

Outstanding items appear as accountability, not as gates. Released weeks never re-lock.

04
Reward behaviour, never reach

No follower counts, likes or views anywhere in the product — not in the ranking, not on a card.

05
Imagery is a placeholder, not decoration

Striped grey blocks with a mono caption mark where real thumbnails, speaker photos and brand assets drop in.

module thumbnail 16:9
06
Accessible at monochrome

Body text ≥ 4.5:1 on its background. Grey #8A8D93 is permitted for labels at 11–12px mono and locked states only.

07

Icon set

One geometric line family on a 24px grid: 1.7px stroke, round caps and joins, currentColor only — an icon inherits the ink of whatever it sits in and never introduces colour. 19px in navigation, 13–16px inline beside mono labels, 28–30px in empty states. Never filled, except the play triangle.
